01About Stone Aura

Every strong structure
begins with a strong
foundation.

Stone Aura has served the construction materials industry since 2014, specialising in the marketing and supply of high-quality stone aggregates and construction materials.

With our own quarry and crusher, we keep closer control over quality, consistency and supply — and deliver reliable materials, competitive pricing and timely service to builders, contractors, developers and infrastructure projects.

From individual requirements to bulk project supplies, we provide the right materials at the right time, so our customers can build with confidence.

01/06
Quarrying
  1. 01

    Stage 01 · Source

    Quarrying

    Granite is extracted from our own quarry, giving us direct control over the raw stone behind every load.

  2. 02

    Stage 02 · Primary

    Primary Crushing

    A jaw crusher reduces large boulders into smaller pieces for further processing.

  3. 03

    Stage 03 · Secondary

    Secondary Crushing

    A cone crusher reduces the stone further, preparing it for the required aggregate sizes.

  4. 04

    Stage 04 · Shaping

    VSI Shaping

    Vertical shaft impact crushing shapes particles into a more cubical form — the “VSI” in our jally and sand.

  5. 05

    Stage 05 · Classification

    Classification

    Air classification separates flaky particles and excess fines for a more uniform manufactured sand.

  6. 06

    Stage 06 · Washing

    Double & Triple Washing

    Washing helps remove excess dust, fines and slurry to produce concrete sand for its intended use.
